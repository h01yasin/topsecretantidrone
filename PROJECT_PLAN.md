## 📋 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Project Overview
Development of an integrated AI-powered anti-drone defense system capable of detecting, tracking, and neutralizing hostile UAVs using multi-sensor fusion technology. The system combines computer vision, RF signal analysis, acoustic detection, radar integration, trajectory prediction, and swarm detection capabilities.

### Key Objectives
1. **Detection:** 99%+ accuracy in identifying drones within 5km range
2. **Response Time:** <5 seconds from detection to threat assessment
3. **Multi-Threat:** Handle up to 20 simultaneous drone targets
4. **Swarm Detection:** Identify coordinated attacks
5. **Counter-Measures:** Automated threat neutralization

### Strategic Value
- **National Defense:** Azerbaijan border security, Karabakh region protection
- **Critical Infrastructure:** Oil facilities (SOCAR), airports, government buildings
- **Commercial:** Private sector security (events, VIPs)
- **Academic:** Research publication, international recognition
- **Industry Partnership:** Defense contractors, military cooperation
